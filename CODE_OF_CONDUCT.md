# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub's private contact options](https://github.com/yusirdemir) or by opening a confidential [security advisory](https://github.com/yusirdemir/contextwatch/security/advisories/new). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful.
